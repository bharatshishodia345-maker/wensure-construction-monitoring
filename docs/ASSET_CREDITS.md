# WENSURE Architectural Asset Credits & Attribution

This document records the visual assets, architectural references, and demonstration imagery used across the WENSURE residential construction oversight platform.

## Ethical Representation Standards

- **Illustrative Demonstration**: All residential imagery shown on the public website and demo portals depicts illustrative contemporary Indian and regional architecture to demonstrate independent civil verification workflows.
- **No Fictitious House Claims**: Generic architectural photography is explicitly labeled as *Illustrative Demo Context* or *Reference Construction Site* and is never falsely identified as a specific audited residence without verified homeowner and auditor consent.
